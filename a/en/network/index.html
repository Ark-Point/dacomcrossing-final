International network

Domestic network

Dual backhaul between Taean and Seoul

Two geographically diverse routes link Taean CLS and Seocho Telehouse to maintain domestic backhaul continuity.

  • Primary 261 km · Taean–Hongseong–Boryeong–Iksan–Daejeon–Telehouse
  • Secondary 480 km · Taean–Pyeongtaek–Osan–Cheonan–Daejeon–Telehouse
  • Seg-F to Taiwan/Hong Kong · Seg-K to Japan
Taean CLS

The Taean gateway connecting Korea and Asia

Taean CLS is the international gateway where subsea cable connects to Korea's terrestrial network. It handles the Korean landing of EAC and international private-line connectivity, supported by dual backhaul and smart monitoring.

  • EAC landing and terrestrial interconnection
  • International circuit intake, branching, and transport
  • Dual backhaul between Taean and Seocho
  • Integrated power, fire, HVAC, and security monitoring
  • 24/365 fault monitoring and field response
