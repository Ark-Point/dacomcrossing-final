International network

EAC, C2C, RNAL, and trans-Pacific networks

EAC-C2C spans approximately 36,800 km across Korea, Japan, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, and the Philippines. EAC-Pacific (UNITY) connects Japan and the U.S. West Coast across approximately 10,000 km.

Domestic network

Dual backhaul between Taean and Seoul

Two geographically diverse routes link Taean CLS and Seocho Telehouse to maintain domestic backhaul continuity.

Taean CLS

The Taean gateway connecting Korea and Asia

Taean CLS is the international gateway where subsea cable connects to Korea's terrestrial network. It handles the Korean landing of EAC and international private-line connectivity, supported by dual backhaul and smart monitoring.

  • EAC landing and terrestrial interconnection
  • International circuit intake, branching, and transport
  • Dual backhaul between Taean and Seocho
  • Integrated power, fire, HVAC, and security monitoring
  • 24/365 fault monitoring and field response
Smart CCMS

Integrated 24-hour facility and security monitoring

Power, fire safety, HVAC, temperature, and humidity are remotely monitored alongside access control, CCTV, and infrared detection.

  • Real-time facility monitoring
  • Access control, CCTV, and infrared security
  • 24/365 operations
Seocho Telehouse

The 24-hour hub where international circuits meet Korea

Seocho Telehouse coordinates with Telstra TxNOC and GSD and the LG U+ Network Control Center to operate subsea, backhaul, and local spans 24/365.

  • Monitoring, planned work, and fault handling
  • EAC backhaul equipment and fiber operations
  • IRU and IPLC activation and testing
  • Customer engineering and cable solutions
  • Sales proposals and technical review
  • Customer notices and incident reports
  • Information security and safety management
