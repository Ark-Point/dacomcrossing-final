FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Find answers about international private-line applications, billing, fault reports, activation, and termination.

Can I change the circuit speed?

The speed can be changed after a technical and commercial review. The monthly charge may change.

How do I apply for service?

Contact 070-5118-3810 or dacom@dacomcrossing.co.kr and our sales team will respond.

How do I set up automatic payment?

Procedures and required documents vary by contract holder and payment method. Contact the sales team.

What is an international private line?

It is a dedicated telecommunications service connecting domestic and overseas offices or data centers.

How do I check service charges?

The sales team can review billing statements, settlement terms, and contract details.

How do I report a fault?

Call 070-5118-3810 at any time. Our team accepts fault reports 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

How long does activation take?

We recommend applying two to three months before the requested activation date.

Is there an early termination fee?

Terms vary by contract and overseas carrier notice period. Consult the sales team in advance.

How do I request termination?

Consult the sales team and submit the required documents.

What information is needed for a quote?

Provide both endpoint addresses, the required speed, and the expected service period.
